**The task, from the end of Lecture 6-2 [25:05]:**
> Find **local news** (internet, print or TV) and **perform the media analysis**. Answer **at least half of the questions**,
> choosing the ones you want, in **at least 3–4 sentences**. Do the writing yourself, not someone else or AI.

So answer **at least 6 of these 11**:

1. Who created the message?
2. What is the purpose of the message?
3. What techniques are used to attract and hold your attention?
4. What values and points of view are represented?
5. What was your reaction to the message?
6. Why did the message make you feel this way?
7. Is your interpretation reasonable and well-informed?
8. How might different people interpret the message?
9. Is the message biased?
10. Is it backed by good reasoning and facts?
11. What are its possible effects on individuals and society?
