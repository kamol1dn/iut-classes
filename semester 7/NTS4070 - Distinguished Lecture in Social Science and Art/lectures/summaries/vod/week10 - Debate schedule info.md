## Timing [01:11]

- Slots start at **10:00** and are about **35 minutes** long. Each slot holds **the two teams that debate each other**,
  ending around 13:35. "We'll see how it goes."
- **If things run late, still arrive on time** and wait until the team before you finishes [01:22].

## If you can't make your slot [01:35]

1. **Arranging a swap is your job, not his.** Agree a change with **the team you're paired with** and with whichever team
   you want to swap days with.
2. **Tell him 1–2 days beforehand** [02:04].
3. **Don't just fail to show up.** If your team won't come, **tell the opposing team too**. Nobody wants to waste the
   morning waiting [02:12].
