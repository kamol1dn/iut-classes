## Two kinds of question [00:50]

1. **Conceptual / definitional.** You're given a concept's name or a definition and pick the matching
   meaning or concept.
2. **Application.** A sentence about a real-life event, an incident, or what
   someone said, and you identify what's going on in it, e.g. **which fallacy they're committing**.
