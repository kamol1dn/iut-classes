## How the debates will run

- **Two sides**: the **proposition** agrees with the topic, and the **opposition** rejects it. Both have to
  argue with valid reasons [02:30]. This is the argument structure from Lecture 5.
- Example: *capitalism* really means "Should our country keep moving towards capitalism, and is it bringing
  prosperity?" [02:10] Most topics are about **Uzbekistan**, and a few are global (AI, for example).
- **Prepare both sides.** Your side is decided by a **coin toss on the day**, when the two teams on the same
  topic meet [06:30]. So if you pick AI, prepare both its benefits and its harms.
- After the survey closes he will **pair teams by topic** and build a **Saturday schedule** for the last
  month of term (weeks 12–14) [06:20].
- A **grading rubric** will come "maybe by midterm" [07:40].
- If a topic is unclear, **message him** [03:20].
