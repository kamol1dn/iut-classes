THE BLOCK PACKER

  a block has room for 4,000,000 weight units · you decide what goes in
  SOC4190 · LECTURE 6




  A payment does not pay a share of what it moves. It pays for the room it takes in a block, and room is measured in
  weight: ordinary bytes count 4, witness bytes count 1. Weigh real payments, compute their fee rate, pack a block
  the way a miner does, and find out what it really costs to get out of the mempool (the waiting room).









The lesson. Weight = 3 × (bytes without witness) + (all bytes); virtual bytes = weight ÷ 4, rounded up to a whole byte; fee rate = fee ÷ vB.
Miners fill the limited room with the highest fee rates first (a simplified model: a real miner also keeps related payments together), so what you pay is your size times the rate of the moment — not a
percentage of the amount. A 30-input payment costs more than a 1-input one, whatever it moves.
